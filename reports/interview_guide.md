# Interview Guide

This project is designed to show a clear, evidence-based workflow in customer churn analysis. The discussion should focus on the methods, assumptions, and practical interpretation of the results.

Key topics to cover:

- Why raw validation matters before modeling.
- How categorical rules and logic checks catch data issues.
- Why churn is an imbalanced target and why accuracy alone is not enough.
- The use of effect sizes and confidence intervals in addition to p-values.
- Why observational data leads to association language instead of causal claims.

The strongest message is that the project starts with validation, then moves into inferential statistics, then predictive modeling, with each step tied back to real evidence from the dataset.
